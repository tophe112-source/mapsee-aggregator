Willow Creek Nature Patch Stewardship Party

Respond

Description

Join PP&R horticulture staff to help keep up the natural garden at Willow Creek Park.

Work parties - weeding, mulching, pruning - are held on Tuesdays, 9:00am - 11:00am, and on 2026-11-07 we plant bulbs.

Questions?

Contact Jane Example at jane.example@portlandoregon.gov or 503-555-0142

If you would like to learn more, please reach out to Robin Sample by phone or email.

For more information, please call 503-555-0100 or email parks@example.org.

If you have questions about the accessibility of the event, please contact us.

  • Bring a water bottle.
  • Gloves and tools are provided.
