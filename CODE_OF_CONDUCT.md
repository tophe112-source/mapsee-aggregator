# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private information are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces.

Source publishers and event organizers are stakeholders too. Respect their
terms, rate limits, removal requests, and decisions not to participate.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to keep the project safe and useful. Serious conduct concerns
may be reported privately to `christophe@conbinience.com`.
